general notes/gameplan:

protect your econ and focus on getting to 8, you want to hit it by stage 17 at the latest.

ideally you will build a critcaster and a tank. ideal tanks are gyarados and lugia, ideal critcasters are sobble and volcanion.

you don't need an aquatic t10/t20. any good critcaster can be a good t10 (flutter mane, cryogonal, etc)

if you need flexibility, aquatic 6 is enough.

    mid: aquatic 6 and 8 are both big power spikes
