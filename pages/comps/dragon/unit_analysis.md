hatches:
    dreepy: range 2, high speed. great physical backliner. 
    axew: generally not worth itemizing beyond the synergy item. still decent as a free 3* dragon. fluffy tail is a trap

add picks:
uncommon and rare add picks help you get dragon 5 in for gyarados

    uncommon:
        skrelp: an important synergy filler for aquatic/dragon.
    rare:
        noibat: high attack speed and range 2, can be a nice midgame carry with physical attacking items
        tyrunt: rock for weather stones is nice, fossil has lots of overlap in dragon. 
    epic:
        swablu: altaria is an important backliner. 
        exeggcute: alolan exeggutor is basically irrelevant. if you have latis, maybe it can help you hit psychic 6. 

uniques:

applin:
    flapple: TODO
    appletun: TODO
    hydrapple: needs good caster items. need to run two turns of gourmet for apples. lose comp and then turn the corner after the mewtwo fight on 14. lucky egg seems mandatory, soul dew, specs, stardust

cyclizar: a bruiser, high attack speed and good durability, really needs damange somehow. muscle band, flame orb + fluffy tail, red orb are all nice.

dracovish: mediocre unit, great synergies. enables a deep aquatic angle, especially if skrelp is added.

dracozolt: mediocre unit, bad synergies. electric is almost never relevant in dragon.

drampa: a backliner. can be somewhat weak in the midgame, but gets stronger as boards get larger late in the game. wants to be a caster. slam water stone. positioning is relevant. it's important to clump your team so that enemies are clumped.

druddigon: fluffy tail is mandatory, so the flame orb fluffy tail combo is best. muscle band or red orb are good. monster 4 is great in the midgame, via bagon and gible or totodile

duraludon: great water stone holder. positioning on the wall is better for the straight line cast.

latios/latias: psychic 4. often  you will itemize one of them and then sell it to itemize your legendary. one dawn stone allows you to hit psychic 6 at level 8. 

turtonator: with charmander, allows fire 8 at level 7 (or level 6 with a fire stone). show example board with water stone, fire 8 dragon 3 water 3


poipole: unplayable after the changes, also possibly bugged? TODO

kecleon: makes dragon 7 water 9 very achievable. a bruiser, likes muscle band.


legendaries:

dialga: deep sea tooth is nice.

eternatus: high speed, likes attack damage items. good wide lens user. poison with skrelp/poipole?

giratina: usually wants to be a backliner. backliner also has flying synergy

kyurem: obviously should always put in frigibax for ice 4. At level 9 you can hit ice 6. example board water 6 ice 6 with shellder and piplup, ice 8 with axew

palkia: easy dragon 7 water 9 with gyarados and horsea.

rayquaza: can be physical or special. the green orb always is worth it if you can get one.

regidrago: go monster 4 with gible + bagon.

reshiram: charmander should make the board.

roaring moon: deino + filler dark unit (or a dark stone?) for dark 5. dark 3 is still worthwhile if you aren't in deino region. seems to want to be a caster, has not performed well as a right clicker for me

walking wake: easy dragon 7 water 9. 

zekrom: a backliner. thunder stone isn't worthwhile. maybe to get electric 5 if you're on dracozolt?

zygarde: much worse in dragon than ground since you won't have any holes dug, meaning it will never transform. it can be ranged, which is nice, but the ranged form's cast isn't great. melee form is more consistent.
